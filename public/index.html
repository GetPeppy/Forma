Core modules

Forma Data Management is the foundation of every bundle. Design Collaboration builds cloud worksharing and coordination on top of it.

📁
Foundation

Forma Data Management

Formerly Autodesk Docs

One connected place for every sheet, model and document on a project, so every team works from the current set through every phase.

What you can do

  • Store and organize drawings, models and documents
  • View 2D and 3D models in the browser
  • Compare versions and mark up sheets
  • Track issues and control permissions by folder
Who uses it: everyone on the project.

An Essentials tier comes with AutoCAD, Revit, Civil 3D and the PD&M Collection, and it's included in all three bundles.

Autodesk naming guide
🤝
Design

Forma Design Collaboration

Formerly BIM Collaborate Pro

Cloud worksharing for Revit, plus tools to share design packages between teams on a timeline and resolve clashes before they reach the site.

What you can do

  • Co-author Revit models in the cloud
  • Publish and consume design packages between teams
  • Run automatic clash detection with Model Coordination
  • Track design changes across disciplines
Who uses it: architects, engineers, BIM and VDC teams.

Included in Forma for Model Management.

Forma bundles

Each bundle packages several products at one price. Products in yellow are the core modules above.

Bundle

Forma for Preconstruction

Formerly the Preconstruction Bundle

Find bidders, level bids, qualify subcontractors and build estimates, so you win more work with less risk.

Included

  • Data Management
  • BuildingConnected Pro
  • Bid Board Pro
  • TradeTapp
  • Forma Takeoff
  • Forma Estimate
  • ProEst

Key products

  • BuildingConnected Pro: bid management with the Builders Network, unlimited invites and bid leveling
  • TradeTapp: subcontractor qualification
  • Takeoff, Estimate, ProEst: quantities and pricing
Who uses it: estimators, precon managers, owners.

Bundle contents can vary by contract. Confirm with your Autodesk rep.

Forma for Model Management

Formerly the Model Management Bundle

Desktop and cloud coordination for VDC: construction-ready models, reality capture, and a digital twin at handover.

Included

  • Data Management
  • Design Collaboration
  • Model Coordination
  • Navisworks Manage
  • ReCap Pro
  • Assemble
  • Tandem Essentials
  • Point Layout
  • Insight

Key products

  • Navisworks Manage: desktop coordination and simulation
  • ReCap Pro: laser scans and photos to 3D
  • Assemble: conditions BIM data for estimating and scheduling
  • Tandem Essentials: digital twin handover
Who uses it: VDC managers, BIM coordinators, trade modelers.

Forma for Construction Operations

Formerly the Construction Operations Bundle

Run the job from kickoff to closeout, with AI help drafting RFIs, building submittal logs and reviewing specs.

Included

  • Data Management
  • Forma Build
  • AutoSpecs
  • Pype Closeout
  • Construction Connect

Key products

  • AutoSpecs: submittal logs generated from specs
